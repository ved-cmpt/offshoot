BILLRITE DATA FILE LAYOUTS

All files are fixed width with one record per line, ASCII, LF line endings.
PIC 9 fields are zero-filled digits with no decimal point; V marks the implied point.
S9(5) fields carry a leading separate sign (+ or -).

CUSTFILE.dat  (100 bytes, copybook CUSTREC, sorted on CUST-ID)
  pos  len  field             picture
    1    8  CUST-ID           X(8)
    9   30  CUST-NAME         X(30)
   39   30  CUST-ADDRESS      X(30)
   69    1  CUST-CLASS        X(1)
   70    1  CUST-SENIOR-FLAG  X(1)
   71    9  CUST-OVERDUE-BAL  9(7)V99
   80    6  CUST-DUE-DATE     9(6)  YYMMDD
   86    7  CUST-CREDIT       9(5)V99
   93    8  FILLER            X(8)
